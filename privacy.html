Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 29, 2026

This policy describes how PDFNest works in its current static website version. If analytics, advertising, accounts or server-side processing are added later, this policy must be updated before those features are enabled.

Document processing: the tools in this version process selected PDF and image files locally in your browser. PDFNest does not send those selected documents to a PDFNest document-processing server.

1. Files you select

Your selected PDF and image files are read by JavaScript running in your browser to perform the task you request. The output is generated in the browser and downloaded to your device. Avoid using a shared device for sensitive files, and keep a copy of the original document.

2. Third-party content delivery networks

PDFNest loads PDF.js, pdf-lib and JSZip from public CDNs so the website can function without bundling a separate server or API. When your browser requests these libraries, the CDN provider may receive ordinary connection information such as your IP address, browser information, and the requested resource. These requests are separate from uploading your document to a PDFNest processing server.

3. Cookies, analytics and advertising

The current version of this site does not intentionally set PDFNest-owned advertising or analytics cookies. If third-party advertising such as Google AdSense is enabled in the future, advertising providers may use cookies or similar technologies to deliver and measure ads, limit repetition, prevent fraud and, where permitted, personalize advertising. This policy must be reviewed and updated to describe the actual vendors and consent requirements before ads are enabled.

For information about Google advertising and available controls, users can review Google's advertising policies and controls. Availability of opt-out controls varies by account, region and device.

4. Contact by email

If you contact us by email, the information you choose to send is handled by your email provider and the recipient mailbox. Email us at udc.officialteam@gmail.com.

5. External links and changes

The website may link to third-party websites. PDFNest does not control their privacy practices. We may update this policy when the website changes; please review this page after significant updates.
